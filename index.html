Where It Comes From

THE SOURCES

Three pillars of modern life driving the pollution crisis.

Industry

Factories and manufacturing plants release tons of particulate matter, VOCs, and toxic chemicals into the atmosphere every single day.

42% of global emissions

Transport

Over 1.4 billion cars worldwide burn fossil fuels, emitting CO₂, nitrogen oxides, and fine particles that penetrate deep into our lungs.

28% of global emissions

Energy

Coal, oil, and gas power plants remain the dominant energy source — churning out sulfur dioxide, heavy metals, and relentless carbon emissions.

30% of global emissions
